\section{Model, Perturbations and Endpoints}
Evidence is tiered: prospective geometry-corrected experiments provide the principal task evidence; archive reanalysis supplies cohort/exposure sensitivity; reconstructed historical selectors are diagnostic only. Missing historical metadata are not inferred from current code.
\subsection{Spiking Substrate and Readout}
The model uses the released signed connectivity and parameters\cite{shiu2024drosophila}, implemented with Brian~2\cite{stimberg2019brian2}. There are 138{,}639 neurons and 15{,}091{,}983 connection-table rows representing 54.5 million contacts. Between events, each unit follows
\begin{equation}
\frac{dv_i}{dt}=\frac{v_0-v_i+g_i}{20\,\mathrm{ms}},
\qquad
\frac{dg_i}{dt}=-\frac{g_i}{5\,\mathrm{ms}} .
\end{equation}
Here $g_i$ is voltage-valued synaptic drive. Events add signed contact counts times $0.275$\,mV after 1.8\,ms. Spikes above $-45$\,mV reset voltage to $v_0=-52$\,mV and drive to zero. Both equations pause during 2.2\,ms refractoriness, retained in sensory cells unlike upstream stimulation. This variant is tested below. Linear integration advances the dynamics; stored initial states are restored before each trial. The time step used in the archived simulations was not documented; fresh runs pin 0.1\,ms.

Independent 150\,Hz Poisson trains drive 104 LC4 and 210 LPLC2 cells (Fig.~\ref{fig:system}). Each external event adds 68.75\,mV to the target voltage. Other cells receive no background drive. LC4/LPLC2 are associated with looming processing, including measured LPLC2 motion opponency\cite{klapoetke2017looming}; the demo's receptive fields are assumptions, not measured maps. The command readout is eight cells of types DNp01, DNa02, DNp04 and DNb01\cite{simpson2023descending}. For duration $T=0.6$\,s, command rate is $r=N_{\mathrm{cmd}}/(8T)$. Latency is the first spike in this group, not a command decision time. Total activity $S$ counts all model spikes.

\subsection{Functional Perturbations}
F1 multiplies each weight by $\exp(\sigma Z-\sigma^2/2)$, $Z\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$, with $\sigma=0.05,0.10,0.20,0.50$. This preserves expected weight; it does not implement bit flips. F2 independently zeroes weighted connection-table rows with probabilities $f=0.05,0.10,0.25,0.50$, not individual contacts; expected lost contact mass is $f$. F3 silences all outgoing connections of a random fraction $0.01,0.02,0.05,0.10$ of eligible units, protecting sensory and command cells. Those units still receive input and spike. F4 targets the top 1\%, 2\% or 5\% by outgoing contact count; its archived selections lack cell manifests and have protection inconsistencies. F5 silences LC4, LPLC2 or both output channels.

There are 12 fault-seeded 600\,ms trials per condition. F1--F3 draw realizations from SHA-256 seeds over (phase, condition, trial). Archived open-loop stimulus streams advance without explicit per-trial reseeding; dispersion combines fault and stimulus variation. Fresh code saves selected cell identities, input hashes and backend metadata. Corrected selection excludes protected FlyWire identities and breaks ties by identity. It has not generated the historical F4 data.

\subsection{Scoring and Controls}
Transmission requires $r\geq\theta=51.4$\,Hz, rounding $(94.5833+8.2292)/2=51.40625$\,Hz from separate ten-trial sensory and random-drive calibrations. The operational joint rule is
\begin{equation}
r\geq51.4\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad r\leq c\,r_{\mathrm H},\qquad S\leq c\,S_{\mathrm H},
\end{equation}
with healthy medians $r_{\mathrm H}=94.6875$\,Hz, $S_{\mathrm H}=39{,}694$ and $c=2$ (sensitivity: 1.5, 3, 4). This operational sensitivity criterion is not a hardware-, power- or safety-validated failure threshold. Joint passes are nested in transmission; disagreement does not establish predictive accuracy. The 288 records mix 216 main-sweep faults and 72 HUB/EIm/random-inhibitory controls, not 288 independent population samples. Twelve healthy references provide resubstitution checks only. Median intervals use 20{,}000 trial resamples, seed 0; relative effects independently resample fault and healthy groups. Dispersion bars instead show 10th--90th percentiles.

The target-permuted null preserves sources, weights, signs and incoming row counts while changing convergence and weighted input. It fails intact transmission, so supplies a specificity control rather than a functioning fault-tolerance alternative. Supplementary Table~S4 reports realized control damage. Expected contact-mass matching does not imply realized edge equality.

\subsection{Stopping Times and Observation Horizons}
Legacy 20\,ms frames couple a readout-driven planar agent to a pursuing threat. Collision is recorded at 0.05\,m with a nominal 1\,s horizon. Six calibration episodes overlap the healthy evaluation; all fault episodes share calibration seeds. We treat these records as illustrative. For paired cumulative spikes $S_{\mathrm F},S_{\mathrm H}$ and recorded durations $T_{\mathrm F},T_{\mathrm H}$, define
\begin{equation}
R_{\mathrm{whole}}=\frac{S_{\mathrm F}(T_{\mathrm F})}{S_{\mathrm H}(T_{\mathrm H})},\quad
R_{\mathrm{common}}=\frac{S_{\mathrm F}(T_*)}{S_{\mathrm H}(T_*)},\quad
R(t)=\frac{S_{\mathrm F}(t)}{S_{\mathrm H}(t)},
\end{equation}
where $T_*=\min(T_{\mathrm F},T_{\mathrm H})$ and $t\leq T_*$. Matching unequal stopping times differs from selecting an earlier horizon. All selected 200--500\,ms windows precede included terminations; full-hub pairs reach 1000\,ms. Diagnostics retain every pair. The $3^3=27$ ordered draws with replacement of three seed clusters are bootstrap resamples, not independent experiments or confirmatory coverage.

\subsection{Prospective Geometric Validation}
The prospective protocol uses body-frame bearing, actual-separation expansion and within-frame collision checks. Nine passive 400\,ms episodes ($-60/-30/0^\circ$, seeds 0--2) fix calibration before testing. At frame $k$, let $d_k$ be summed left-minus-right command rates and $a_k$ their eight-cell mean. Bars denote means over the latest five frames (all available frames initially). Steering is
\begin{equation}
\omega_k=-400\,\mathrm{deg/s}\;\operatorname{clip}\!\left(\frac{\bar d_k-b}{\ell},-1,1\right),
\label{eq:steering}
\end{equation}
with frozen $b=-20$\,Hz, $\ell=70$\,Hz and positive data sign. Body-frame $+30^\circ$ is left, $-30^\circ$ right; positive heading turns left. For example, $\bar d=50$\,Hz gives $\omega=-400^\circ$/s (right), whereas $\bar d=-90$\,Hz gives $+400^\circ$/s (left). These define the implemented sign convention, not a biological response. Triggering requires current $\bar a_k$ and preceding unsmoothed $a_{k-1}$ both to reach 18.125\,Hz. The first qualifying frame schedules one burst 250\,ms after its end. Burst speed is 0.35\,m/s for 450\,ms with fixed heading; otherwise speed is 0.05\,m/s and heading is capped at $\pm100^\circ$. Cue onset is 100\,ms; original steering has no onset gate.

The first 48 tests use seeds 6--11, $\pm30^\circ$, a 1\,s horizon, healthy/F2\_25/F2\_50 and readout disabling. Healthy controls on seeds 12--17 compare the original law, pre-cue gating ($\omega=0$ before 100\,ms), offset only ($\bar d=0$) and no steering ($\omega=0$), retaining the neural trigger. The new factorial comparison reuses 24 byte-identical original/gated healthy records and adds 48 F2\_25/F2\_50 episodes on these same seeds and bearings. Calibration, neural-source snapshots, input hashes and package versions are verified unchanged. Brian2 seed $20000+s$ and deletion seed $10000+s$ are reset per episode. Masks are shared across controllers/bearings and nested across doses. Healthy outcomes were known before this extension; no controller is retuned.

For matched binary survival $Y_{\mathrm H},Y_{\mathrm F}$, paired loss is $L=I(Y_{\mathrm H}=1,Y_{\mathrm F}=0)$, gain is $G=I(Y_{\mathrm H}=0,Y_{\mathrm F}=1)$ and net loss $D=Y_{\mathrm H}-Y_{\mathrm F}=L-G$. The interaction is $D_{\mathrm{gate}}-D_{\mathrm{original}}$, averaging both bearings within seed. Exploratory conditional ranges use 20{,}000 six-cluster resamples, seed~0; 72 records are not independent replicates. Activity compares each faulty record to the same-law healthy reference at 200--500\,ms and common duration. The NumPy backend uses Brian2~2.10.1, NumPy~2.5.4 and 0.1\,ms steps on a Xeon W-2223. Supplementary Tables~S17--S22 give paired records, activity, thresholds and compute; protocol v8 gives reproduction commands.
