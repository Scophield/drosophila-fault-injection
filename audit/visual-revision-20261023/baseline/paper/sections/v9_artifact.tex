\section{Executable Evidence and Audit Cost}
The evidence interface records one seed/bearing/law/fault cell per JSON file, with complete frame and actuator-segment traces. Maps, calibration, mask/input-event digests and generating snapshots bind to external-table hashes. Analysis rejects incomplete matrices or mixed bindings. Independent replay implements geometry, decisions and input reconstruction without importing the task implementation or loading the connectome; separate arithmetic checks reconstruct cluster effects and warning denominators. In-memory mutation tests reject inconsistent headings, burst intervals and horizon flags, and verify prefix metrics are unchanged by future outcomes. A tiny Brian~2 test verifies episode-state and fault-weight restoration.

The core neural campaign takes 14,393\,s with three CPU workers; median episode wall time is 145.5\,s. Independent trace/input replay takes 34.4\,s. These perform different work: replay consumes recorded neural outputs and does not certify neural-state correctness or establish a simulation speedup. No hardware energy or area is inferred.

The matched assessment-v9 source/data package supplies raw JSON/CSV, generating snapshots, registrations, TeX, figures and checksums; external connectivity tables remain separate. Registrations are local, not externally timestamped. A logged post-registration correction only separates numeric-margin and Boolean-warning CSV field names; frozen sources and raw records remain intact. Reanalysis/replay requires no new neural execution. Public main alone may predate this revision; the matched package is required.
