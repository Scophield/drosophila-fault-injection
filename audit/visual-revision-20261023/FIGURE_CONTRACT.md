statistics are unchanged. The preceding PDFs and all active sources/figures
are preserved in baseline/; the named v9 PDFs and scientific ZIP stay intact.

The preceding revision removed too much visual explanation: a generic box
pipeline obscured the model/body interface, while twelve large bars repeated
binary outcome summaries without showing paired seeds, motion or effect sizes.
Recover the older manuscript's concrete biological/task narrative using v9 data.

1. **Model-to-task overview**, schematic-led composite, 3.5 x 2.9 inches.
   Show the visual-input/model/descending-readout/body loop, a labeled pursuit
   geometry and causal clocks. All circuit icons and geometry are schematic;
   no synthetic trace is passed off as a measurement. Show only current F2 row
   deletion, not obsolete five-family experiments.
2. **Feedback and task loss**, asymmetric mixed-modality figure, 7.16 x 2.9 inches.
   A real matched trajectory example restores physical meaning. Its seed is
   selected descriptively as the first registered negative-bearing P50 failure,
   not as a representative or independent test. A complete 24-row/12-column
   outcome matrix prevents selective examples. A forest panel shows the same
   registered two dose contrasts with whole-seed intervals, Holm p and the
   unequal healthy reserve qualification. No new significance tests.
3. **Early warning**, quantitative comparison, single-column.
   Show held-out neural scores against the later binary task outcome, and all
   frozen criteria's false-alarm/detection trade-off. Every point and interval
   comes from the existing CSV/summary. Exact geometry's information advantage
   and the 13-positive/275-negative denominators remain explicit.
