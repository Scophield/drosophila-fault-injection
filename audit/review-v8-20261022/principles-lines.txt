The original law's zero net loss at this dose contains one loss and one gain, not absence of fault effects. Six clusters, directional floors, negative/frontal calibration coverage and assumed receptive fields limit generalization. Some survivors are still bursting at 1\,s; bounded survival does not establish eventual escape.
