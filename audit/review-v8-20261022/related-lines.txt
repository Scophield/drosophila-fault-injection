1: \section{Related Work}
2: Connectome models connect anatomy to executable sensorimotor hypotheses\cite{shiu2024drosophila}, while graph statistics characterize wiring rather than dynamical reliability\cite{lin2024network}. A recent sparsification study investigates compression across connection strength, placement, state and task, including active-state over-excitation\cite{an2026sparse}. Endpoint-dependent damage responses have also been reported on the male central nervous system connectome\cite{jo2026simulating}. Those studies investigate how damage or sparsification changes function. Our assessment comparison explicitly separates paired exposure, full observation horizons and neural versus task endpoints. It introduces no perturbation operator or claim of superior fault tolerance. Bilateral integration measured in the giant-fiber circuit\cite{jang2023azimuthal} cautions against treating the controller's directional failure as established fly behavior.

4: SpikeFI\cite{spyrou2024spikefi}, SpikingJET\cite{gogebakan2024spikingjet} and enpheeph\cite{colucci2022enpheeph} provide SNN fault-injection frameworks. Population-coded regression\cite{gleyo2026population} and scientific computing\cite{theilman2026robustness} assess application outputs. Loihi~2 deployment\cite{wang2025neuromorphic} establishes implementation feasibility. NeuroBench\cite{yik2023neurobench} and embodied benchmarking\cite{dangelo2026embodied} motivate separate functional/resource measures. We examine assessment definitions rather than benchmark injector implementations.
