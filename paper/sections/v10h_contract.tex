\section{Executable Evidence Contract}
A campaign declares seed, condition, law, fault, horizon $H$, frame duration and resource unit. Adapters normalize native traces into those identities, source/map/calibration bindings, input/mask digests, completed observations, cumulative resource and first failure. Hashes bind bytes, not execution. Common checks test matrix membership, pairing, clocks, exposure, resources and outcome labels; native replay separately regenerates inputs and plant transitions. Shared variates need not produce identical feedback-dependent events. Warning prefixes exclude future labels.

Core field types and key hashability are validated before regex/set operations. The reproduced null digest, numeric digest and list seed now yield row-indexed schema issues; valid rows continue and missing cells remain reported. This guards the tested normalized schema, not arbitrary native semantics.

\reuseRef\ uses the same checker on a distinct delayed tank: $\dot h=I-u-0.04h$, $h(0)=0.5$, safe interval $[0.2,0.8]$. Eight seeds cross two inflows, two fixed laws and three sensor-deletion levels (0/25/50\%), producing 96 examples. Deleted samples hold their last value; an independent analytic replayer checks sensor accounting and queued actuation, delayed 150\,ms on a 100-ms observation grid. All examples remain safe: they demonstrate reuse, not neural fault tolerance.

The original challenge set detects eleven common-checker and three native-only cases per adapter, not general coverage. Historical clock/actuator defects remain separate regression fixtures. A synthetic zero-neural-readout logger passes input/plant replay without solving neural equations: consistent traces cannot authenticate execution, unlogged state or safety.
