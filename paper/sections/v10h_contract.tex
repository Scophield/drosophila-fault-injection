\section{Executable Evidence Contract}
Adapters normalize native traces, binding campaign identities, clocks, resource units/histories, sources, maps, calibration, input/mask digests and first failure (Fig.~\ref{fig:v10f-system}(d)). Shared variates need not yield equal feedback-dependent event hashes; prefixes exclude future labels.

The second plant obeys $\dot h=I-u-0.04h$, $h(0)=0.5$, safe interval $[0.2,0.8]$. Eight seeds, two inflows, two laws and three sensor-deletion levels give 96 safe examples; missing samples hold their last value (\reuseRef(a)). Native replay checks analytic transitions and 150-ms actuation on a 100-ms grid (\reuseRef(b)).

Before scoring, we froze 111 software case IDs: 87 corruptions and 24 valid IDs (107 distinct inputs; 20 distinct valid inputs), not independent statistical samples. Valid controls retain off-grid actuation, worker partitions and feedback-dependent histories. This author-designed catalogue is not blind generalization evidence.

Arm A checks schema, matrix membership and trusted input/configuration/mask headers, not whole-record hashes. B adds unchanged common pairing, clock, exposure, cumulative-resource and label invariants; C adds native input/actuator/plant and seeded-mask replay; D combines them. All arms use identical normalized rows without short-circuiting; registry setup is timed separately.
