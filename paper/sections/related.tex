\section{Related Work}
The release of complete wiring diagrams for the adult \emph{Drosophila} brain\cite{lin2024network} has shifted connectome research from static graph analysis to executable models spanning visual system convolutions\cite{liew2026connectomebased}, neuron-level response prediction\cite{xie2025visual}, learned tasks and whole-body locomotion on whole-CNS wiring\cite{cho2026drosophila,senge2026connectome,jin2026wholebrain}, and biofidelic processing units\cite{yu2025biological}, with cell-type studies resolving descending-neuron input sharing\cite{zhang2026synaptic,drummond2026population}. Closest to our substrate, the released LIF whole-brain model (footnote 1) reproduces global activity and has been ported to Loihi~2\cite{wang2025neuromorphic}. None of these works varies the integrity of the controller; we treat the connectome controller as a fixed plant and sweep internal fault modes.

Graph-theoretic lesion studies score the structural attack tolerance of brain networks\cite{aerts2016brain}, not dynamics; spiking-network weight-noise robustness is mostly characterized on small trained architectures\cite{thieu2022effects,yunusoglu2025neuromorphic}, with adaptive mitigation now studied at the edge\cite{luca2026learned}; purpose-built fault-injection frameworks for trained SNNs---SpikeFI\cite{spyrou2024spikefi}, SpikingJET\cite{gogebakan2024spikingjet}---automate this but score task accuracy on human-designed networks, continued by STDP campaigns and population-coded redundancy\cite{jouni2025stdptrained,gleyo2026population}. Hardware platforms expose complementary constraints: on-chip variability co-design\cite{arfa2025hardwareaware}, FPGA precision--capacity trade-offs\cite{gautam2025neurocorex}, permanent interconnect faults\cite{chessa2026crosslayer}, edge energy--robustness envelopes\cite{imanov2026energyefficient,katti2026neuromorphic}, and trained-CNN dependability\cite{graciamorn2026dependability}. Benchmarking efforts omit fault-injection axes\cite{yik2023neurobench}, and formalizations of neuromorphic control do not yet treat degradation\cite{medvedeva2025formalizing}. Insect-inspired collision-aversion circuits are robust by construction, but small and hand-designed\cite{shuang2023opplod,qin2025dynamic}. What a complete connectome uniquely enables is tolerance measurement at the scale of an entire evolved controller; these measurements suggest hypotheses for subsequent hardware validation.
