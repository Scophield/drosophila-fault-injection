\section{Scope, Artifacts and Conclusion}
The interface adds executable checks for declared pairing, causal timing and exposure across two native schemas; it does not prove arbitrary fault coverage or authentic neural execution. Neural evidence remains conditional on one graph, an assumed RF family, a synthetic plant, unmatched healthy reserve and sparse failures. Walk-only contact by 0.8\,s follows from this plant's speed construction, not connectome-specific advantage. Functional row deletions establish no biological tolerance, physical upset rate, hardware safety, energy or area benefit.

The matching V10 source and saved-record package is prepared for versioned public access; the V9 parent alone does not identify it. Saved-data replay requires no external matrices; fresh neural simulation requires separately obtained, hash-matched inputs. Third-party tables with unresolved redistribution terms are excluded from the public layer. A fresh checkout and clean dependency installation previously reproduced 448 V9 saved traces on the same host, without establishing second-host neural replication. New RF tests used four Xeon W-2223 workers, about 179\,s median episode wall time and at most 1.21\,GiB lifetime working set per worker.

Checked evidence and independent RF tests support a bounded engineering conclusion: valid pairing and clocks make conditional fault results interpretable, while neither preserved activity nor a successful baseline establishes a universal protection policy.
