\section{Results and Conditional Interpretation}
\subsection{RF Transfer Weakens the Baseline Law Contrast}
Fig.~\ref{fig:v10f-transfer} reports primary loss counts and whole-seed uncertainty. All healthy tasks succeed, and no gains occur. The baseline 50\%-dose secondary contrast is $-22.9$\,pp (95\% CI $[-33.3,-12.5]$; Holm $p=0.00195$). Both new contrasts are $-4.2$\,pp, CI $[-12.5,0]$; all four new map/dose tests have Holm $p=1$. Each new 50\% contrast has only one nonzero cluster. These data neither precisely reproduce the large baseline advantage nor establish equivalence. The baseline 25\% contrast remains uncertain ($p=0.50$); no 25\% loss is observed in A/B. With twelve event-free clusters, the one-sided 95\% upper bound for any loss in a new identically defined two-bearing cluster is 22.1\%, not zero trial risk.

All four new failures are retained: A has P/50\% contacts at seed 3011/$-30^\circ$ and 3009/$+30^\circ$, plus R/50\% at 3009/$+30^\circ$; B has P/50\% at 3110/$-30^\circ$. Healthy minimum clearance and executed heading differ between laws. RF-0 median P/R clearance is 33.1/41.4\,mm, with absolute heading at 400\,ms of $88.3^\circ/100^\circ$. This whole-system comparison does not isolate fault-specific tolerance from healthy reserve. Separate map calibrations and test cohorts further preclude attributing the weaker contrasts solely to RF changes.

\subsection{Warning Errors and Exposure Limits}
\WarningFigure
\warningRef\ shows that neural warnings retain misses and false alarms. Command criteria detect 2/13, 2/3 and 0/1 contacts in RF-0/A/B, respectively. The upper-only activity rule detects none in any map. The exact-state held-command forecast detects all observed contacts but flags 57/275, 25/141 and 33/143 successful tasks. Baseline schedule-only false alarms are 84/275. New sensitivities have only two/one failure clusters; 2,217/7,099 of 20,000 resamples lack a positive class. Intervals omit those draws only for the corresponding conditional rate. Zero-miss empirical intervals do not establish deployable protection.

Own-stop/common-duration medians coincide in all three maps, with maximum paired differences below 0.006. The historical large stopping-time effect does not recur. Fixed windows still differ: P/50\% median 400-ms/1-s ratios are 0.981/1.505, 0.961/1.212 and 0.956/1.139. The separate eight-cluster $\pm45^\circ$ test flags 22/32 healthy activity records and has an uncertain 50\% law contrast. The historical six-cluster 25\% post-hoc $p=0.25$ remains separate, without confirmatory reinterpretation.
