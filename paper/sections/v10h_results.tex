\subsection{First-Contact Loss Depends on the Declared Horizon}
Fig.~\ref{fig:v10f-transfer}(a) reports paired losses; Fig.~\ref{fig:v10f-transfer}(d) rescores the same 50\%-deletion trajectories at 900/1000\,ms across all RFs. RF-0 has nine additional P contacts in the final 100\,ms (latest 999.962\,ms); law-discordant RF-A/B contacts also occur after 900\,ms. These descriptive rescorings add neither samples nor confirmatory tests; RF-0's repeated endpoints were registered.

The prespecified secondary R-minus-P net-loss contrast (Fig.~\ref{fig:v10f-transfer}(b)) was $-22.9$\,pp (95\% CI $[-33.3,-12.5]$; Holm $p=0.00195$). A/B each yielded $-4.2$\,pp, CI $[-12.5,0]$ (all four transfer Holm $p=1$; one nonzero seed per 50\% contrast). This establishes neither equivalence nor an isolated RF effect; no between-map test was run. RF-0/25\% remains uncertain ($p=0.50$). A/B have no 25\% loss; twelve event-free clusters permit a one-sided 95\% upper bound of 22.1\% for any loss in a new two-bearing cluster.

All 576 records triggered. Post-hoc fault-minus-healthy trigger-time differences at 50\% deletion averaged 80.0/57.5/57.5\,ms in RF-0/A/B (SD 42.9/46.6/29.7\,ms across 48/24/24 bearing pairs; 24/12/12 independent seeds, P/R duplicates counted once). Trigger means first post-cue crossing by the eight-cell five-frame mean at 20-ms resolution. All 288 P/R pairs shared trigger/burst schedules under pre-trigger gating, yet 15 had different contact outcomes. Both RF-0 25\% P contacts occurred without delay: timing alone neither determines outcome across laws nor accounts for every contact.

Among 543 records with no contact by burst completion and positive follow-up, all 3,114 decisions at/after burst end and before contact/$H$ had zero eight-cell features and gated turn; 474 retained whole-network spikes. Thirteen later contacts fall in this observed walking regime, not a universal silence rule or isolated delay mechanism. Exclusions are four prior contacts, 17 ongoing bursts and 12 zero-follow-up records.

Fig.~\ref{fig:v10f-transfer}(c) shows paired healthy minimum-clearance points and vertical median marks: RF-0 P/R medians 33.1/41.4\,mm; 400-ms absolute headings $88.3^\circ/100^\circ$. The contrast compares complete controllers with unequal healthy reserve. Burst-end state and follow-up are possible contributors; RF calibration and cohorts vary together, precluding isolated mechanisms.
