P1 and P2 follow from the flat F1 and F3 medians: computation lives in distributed convergence, consistent with hardware observations\cite{arfa2025hardwareaware,thieu2022effects}. P3: disconnection costs are latency; real-time loops should budget Fig.~\ref{fig:curves}a. P4: hub silencing is the only \emph{amplifying} mode; monitor envelope violation, not liveness. P5: each sensory channel alone suffices (68--79\% median), marking the sensor interface as the first redundancy candidate.

Identical relative pathway decay yet only the biological graph transmitting a decodable command, argues against certifying embodied controllers by topology alone; fault-injection campaigns belong in pre-deployment stages\cite{yik2023neurobench,medvedeva2025formalizing}.

\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
Our LIF abstraction (single compartments, no plasticity, Poisson drive) fixes one drive rate; doses kept the background regime intact, the hub analysis is descriptive, and correlated damage and recovery are excluded. Under the tested drive, the median command rate is preserved under weight noise and unit silencing and retains 76.5\% at 50\% loss; the dangerous failure is hub-silencing over-activity, an ordering a preliminary synthetic loop reproduces.
