\section{Scope, Artifacts and Conclusion}
Evidence concerns one graph, an assumed RF family, a synthetic plant, unequal healthy reserve and sparse failures. Roughly $66^\circ$ held-out RMSE limits bearing reconstruction. Walk-only contact by 0.8\,s reflects plant speeds; one burst followed by slower walking makes 1-s success finite-horizon preservation, not sustained avoidance. Functional row loss establishes no biological tolerance, device upset rate, hardware safety or energy/area benefit; trace consistency cannot authenticate neural execution.

Code and data are fixed\cite{artifactEngineering}; neural reruns require hash-matched upstream matrices.

We quantify conditional task preservation and record-consistency gains. This task-level assessment provides a basis for evaluating how future device-level nonidealities propagate through neuromorphic controllers to closed-loop behavior.
